Code Infinity IT Solutions was founded in 2026 by Manikandan, with a vision to build world-class digital products right from Tamil Nadu. What started as a small team of passionate developers has grown into a full-spectrum technology company trusted by clients across India and internationally.

The name "Code Infinity" reflects our core belief — that the possibilities of technology are truly infinite. Every challenge has a solution. Every idea can be built. Every business can be transformed through the right application of technology.

Today, we are a team of 12+ engineers, designers, and strategists delivering exceptional digital experiences across web, mobile, cloud, and AI.
