∞ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?+

It depends on scope. A simple landing page takes 1–2 weeks. A full SaaS web app typically takes 8–16 weeks. We'll give you an accurate timeline in your free consultation before we begin.

Do you work with startups or only enterprises?+

Both! We love working with early-stage startups who need to move fast, and enterprises who need reliable, scalable systems. Our team adapts to your stage and needs.

What is your development process?+

We follow an agile sprint model — Discovery → Design → Development → Testing → Deployment. You get weekly demos and can provide feedback at every stage. No surprises, full transparency.

Do you provide post-launch support?+

Absolutely. All our packages include at least 1 month of free post-launch support. We also offer ongoing maintenance plans for teams who want us to manage their infrastructure long-term.

Can I see the code and own it after delivery?+

Yes — 100% of the code we write for you is yours. We deliver full source code via GitHub and you retain complete ownership. No lock-in, ever.
